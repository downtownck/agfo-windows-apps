Windows paketi (AGFO-System-Care-vX-Windows.zip) hazir calisma ortami olarak asagidaki acik kaynak bilesenleri
ayri dosyalar halinde (_internal klasoru) icerir. Lisans metinleri ilgili projelerde yer alir:

- Python 3.12 (Python Software Foundation License)            https://www.python.org/
- Tcl/Tk (BSD tarzi lisans)                                      https://www.tcl-lang.org/
- ttkbootstrap 2.2.7 (MIT)                                        https://github.com/israel-dryer/ttkbootstrap
- Pillow (MIT-CMU / HPND)                                         https://github.com/python-pillow/Pillow
- Paramiko (LGPL-2.1; degistirilmeden, ayri modul olarak)         https://github.com/paramiko/paramiko
- cryptography (Apache-2.0 / BSD)                                 https://github.com/pyca/cryptography
- bcrypt, PyNaCl (Apache-2.0)                                     https://github.com/pyca
- pypdf (BSD-3-Clause)                                            https://github.com/py-pdf/pypdf
- PyInstaller onyukleyicisi (GPL-2.0, onyukleyici istisnasiyla)   https://pyinstaller.org/

Istege bagli, pakete dahil olmayan ve kullanicinin onayiyla WinGet uzerinden kurulan:
- LibreHardwareMonitor (MPL-2.0) — sensorler                      https://github.com/LibreHardwareMonitor/LibreHardwareMonitor
